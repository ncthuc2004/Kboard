# Kboard v1.1.12 Release Notes

## Toi Uu & Tu Dong Dong Bo IP Khi 2 May Chung Mang Wi-Fi

### 1. Tu Dong Ket Noi May Nhan Trong Cung Mang LAN
- **Tu dong bat song & ket noi**: Khi 2 may cung ket noi vao mot mang Wi-Fi (hoac Hotspot), may ban phim khi quet thay may nhan duy nhat se **tu dong ket noi ngay lap tuc** ma nguoi dung khong can phai bam chon thu cong.
- **Loai bo su nham lan ve dia chi IP**:
  - Giao dien thanh ket noi tren may ban phim gio day hien thi ro rang ca 2 dia chi:
    `May nay: <IP_May_Ban_Phim> -> <IP_May_Nhan>:8964 [Doi]`
  - Giup nguoi dung nhin thay truc quan: 2 may cung nam trong cung dai IP mang Wi-Fi (vi du may ban phim la `192.168.1.20`, may nhan la `192.168.1.15`), hoan toan binh thuong theo tieu chuan mang may tinh quoc te.

### 2. Canh Bao Lech Lop Mang (Subnet Matching)
- Truong hop may ban phim va may nhan vo tinh ket noi vao 2 mang khac nhau (vi du 1 may dang bat 4G hoac o mang Wi-Fi khac):
  - Thanh cong cu se hien thi nhan canh bao mau vang: `Khac dai IP • Hay [Quet LAN]`.
  - Giup nguoi dung phat hien ngay su bat dong bo mang de ket noi lai cung mot Wi-Fi.

### 3. Tich Hop Nut Bat Tat Nhanh Hotspot
- Khi may ban phim nhan dien dang ket noi vao dai phat song di dong (`192.168.43.x`), he thong lap tuc hien nut bam nhanh `[Dung Hotspot]` de tro ve `192.168.43.1:8964` chi voi 1 thao tac.
